#definition 
Unicast is point-to-point communication where a single sender transmits data directly to a single specific recipient on a network.

- uses a specific [[IP]] or [[MAC]]
- [[switches|Switches]] forward known unicast frames out **only one port** (or flood them if the MAC is unknown). See [[MAC address table]].
- [[routing|Routers]] route unicast packets by looking up the destination in the routing table.
