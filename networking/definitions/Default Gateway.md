#networking #definition 

A __default gateway__  is the default router that an device must use to send packets to destinations that are outside of the network. it is also known as a default route it is a route to `0.0.0.0/0` this is the least specific route that could be made.

- traffic is sent to the default route when no more specific route can be used. 
- it is often used to direct traffic to the internet
